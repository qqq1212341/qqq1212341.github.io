Privacy Policy

This policy explains how privacy information for Dumb Game Lab mobile games will be presented. Game-specific details are added before a game is released or when its data practices change.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to mobile games published by Dumb Game Lab (DGL), an independent game studio based in Gwacheon-si, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea. Individual games may provide an additional policy or disclosure where needed.

2. Game-specific disclosures

Each game’s privacy information will identify the data practices relevant to that game, including any information collected by the game, the reason it is used, retention details where applicable, and choices available to players.

3. Analytics and advertising

If a game uses analytics, advertising, or other third-party services, that game’s disclosure will name the provider and describe the relevant data practices. No provider is listed here until it is configured for a specific game.

4. Children’s privacy

Any game intended for children or offered in a context with child-specific privacy requirements will include the disclosures and controls applicable to that game.

5. Updates

We may update this policy as our games or their data practices change. The current version will be available on this page.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, use the official company email shown on the Dumb Game Lab contact section. The address is configured before public release.
